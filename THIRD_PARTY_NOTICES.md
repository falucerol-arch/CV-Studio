## Reactive Resume template adaptations

CV Studio's 18 template variants (Azurill, Bronzor, Chikorita, Ditgar, Ditto, Gengar, Glalie, Kakuna, Lapras, Leafish, Meowth, Onyx, Persian, Pikachu, Porygon, Rhyhorn, Scizor, and Smeargle) are independent adaptations of visual layout ideas from the PDF templates in [Reactive Resume](https://github.com/reactive-resume/reactive-resume), by Amruth Pillai. The upstream project is licensed under the MIT License. The first three represent its two-column left, one-column ATS, and two-column right layout families. CV Studio retains its own HTML/CSS preview, data model, and `@react-pdf/renderer` exporter; it does not bundle Reactive Resume's Forme renderer or template source components.
